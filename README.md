# Global Tech Layoffs Analysis --- Power BI

Interactive Power BI dashboard exploring global tech layoffs by time,
company, industry, country, and funding stage.

## Dashboard Pages

-   **Executive Overview:** Key KPIs, monthly trends, layoff
    classification, yearly trends, and top companies.
-   **Industry & Company Analysis:** Layoffs by industry and company,
    industry contribution, and yearly comparisons.
-   **Geographic & Funding Analysis:** Country map, country comparisons,
    and layoffs by funding stage.
-   **Company Deep Dive:** Company-level layoff events, yearly/monthly
    trends, and event-size breakdown.
-   **Insights:** Key findings and business recommendations.

## Key Findings

-   **2023 was the peak year:** 264,220 employees were laid off, up
    around 60% from 2022.
-   **January 2023 was the highest-layoff month:** 89,709 employees.
-   **Layoffs were concentrated:** The top 10 companies accounted for
    26.4% of total layoffs.
-   **Largest company totals:** Intel (38,115), Microsoft (30,013), and
    Amazon (27,940).
-   **Large events drove the totals:** Mega-layoff events were few but
    represented a substantial share of employees laid off.

## Tools & Dataset

-   **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI
-   **Dataset:** [Global Tech Layoffs Analysis ---
    Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blixture/global-tech-layoffs-analysis-dataset/data)
-   **Report file:** `TechLayoff.pbix`



## Business Recommendations

-   Monitor industries and companies with repeated or large layoff
    events.
-   Track event size as well as total layoffs to identify unusual
    spikes.
-   Consider country concentration when interpreting global trends.
-   Treat funding and layoffs as a correlation to explore, not proof of
    cause and effect.
